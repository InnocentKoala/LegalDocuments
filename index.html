1. What Information We DO NOT Collect

To ensure complete user safety and privacy:

  • No Passwords: GrowthPulse never asks for, records, or stores your Instagram account password.
  • No Exported Session Tokens: We do not export your Instagram session cookies or authentication tokens to external servers.
  • No Private Messages: We do not read, access, or store your Instagram Direct Messages (DMs).
  • No Off-Site History: GrowthPulse operates strictly on authorized Instagram web pages and does not track your general browsing activity.

2. Information We Collect and Store Locally

All engagement preferences and operational data are saved locally on your device using Chrome's secure storage (chrome.storage.local):

  • User Preferences: Your configured target accounts, minimum/maximum likes per lead, and daily quotas.
  • Pacing Telemetry: Hourly speed maps, cooldown timestamps, and local performance counters.
  • Lead Buffers: Temporary queues of publicly visible usernames queued for interaction.

3. Account & Commercial Licensing (Whop)

If you purchase a paid license or activate a bonus tier:

  • License Keys: Validated securely through our merchant partner, Whop Inc. (whop.com).
  • Email Address: If provided during purchase or receipt requests, your email is used solely for order fulfillment and customer support.
  • Public Handle: Your connected Instagram username is resolved locally to maintain single-user license validation.

4. Data Sharing & Third Parties

We do NOT sell, rent, or trade your personal data to any third party.

External services are strictly limited to necessary infrastructure:

  • Whop Inc.: Payment processing and license validation.
  • Google Firebase: Secure remote configuration for engine safety thresholds.
